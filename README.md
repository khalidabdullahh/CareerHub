## 🛰️ Executive Summary & Concept

**[FreeAICV.me](https://www.freeaicv.me)** is an enterprise-grade AI career platform engineered to solve the broken global job application pipeline. 

Over 75% of submitted resumes are discarded by Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) due to incompatible multi-column tables, unreadable graphics, or missing contextual keywords. FreeAICV solves this through a **two-tier architecture**:

1. **30 Recruiter-Calibrated ATS Vector Layouts** guaranteeing flawless parsing across Workday, Greenhouse, Taleo, Lever, and iCIMS.
2. **A 17-Tool Automated AI Career Suite** powered by Google Gemini AI that writes, scores, audits, and tailors career documents in real time.
